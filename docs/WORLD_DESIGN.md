# World Design

- **Central Kingdom:** Hub area
- **Forest of Awakening (Phase 3 Playable Vertical Slice):**
  - Dense mystical woodland spanning $2000 \times 2000$ world space.
  - **The Corrupted Grove:** Centered at coordinates $(450, 450)$.
    - Initial State: Swirling purple mist, withered dark purple trees, corruption particles, and guarded by the Corrupted Grove Beast.
    - Cleansed State: After defeating the beast and activating the Harmony Crystal with `[E]`, the dark corruption dissipates, trees transform into lush vibrant green blooming foliage, and golden wildflowers blossom across the clearing!
  - **Harmony Crystal:** Positioned in the grove clearing at $(620, 460)$. Emanates celestial harmonic light and restores health/mana to full.
- **Forest of Arrays:** Arrays, Searching (Phase 4)
- **The Chained Cave:** Linked Lists (Phase 5)
- **Tower of Stacks:** Stacks (Advanced)
- **The Queue Kingdom:** Queues
- **The Sorting Arena:** Sorting Algorithms
- **The Tree of Knowledge:** Trees
- **The Graph City:** Graphs
